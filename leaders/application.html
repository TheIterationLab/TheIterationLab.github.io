05 Platform Policies

Community & Conduct Policy

  • Leaders must treat all members with respect regardless of background, institution, or skill level.
  • Plagiarism, fabrication of data, or misrepresentation of research is strictly prohibited and grounds for immediate removal.
  • All published work under The Iteration Lab must follow the platform's citation and attribution guidelines.
  • Leaders may not use their position to promote personal commercial interests within the platform.
  • Confidential member information shared through the platform must not be disclosed to outside parties.
  • Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any kind will result in immediate termination of leadership status.

Activity & Engagement Policy

  • Leaders are expected to remain active on the platform — contributing to research discussions, reviews, or publishing at least once per month.
  • Consistent inactivity in both research publishing and community socialising may be interpreted as disengagement, and the application or leadership role may be cancelled at the discretion of the governance authority.
  • Leaders must respond to governance communications within 7 days.
  • Any planned absence longer than 30 days must be notified in advance to the platform admin.
Notice on Removal of Leadership Position: The governance authority of The Iteration Lab reserves the unconditional right to suspend or permanently revoke any leadership appointment at any time, in the event of a policy violation, breach of community standards, or sustained inactivity. Furthermore, a pending application may be declined or a granted position may be cancelled if the applicant is not sufficiently connected to the platform through active publishing or community engagement. Decisions made by the governance authority are final.
